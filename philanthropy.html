Her Foundation

Haddassah International Foundation

Inspired by her faith and love for humanity, Lady Pastor Maame Akua Obeng Darko founded the Haddassah Foundation to bring love, hope, and practical support to people and communities in need.

The foundation stands beside the overlooked — the widow, the child, the family in hardship — offering not only assistance, but dignity. Every act of service is an expression of the conviction that faith must be visible in love.

“When we serve the least of these, we serve Him.”
